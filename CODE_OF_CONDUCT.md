# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project and our community a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, friendly, safe, and constructive environment.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing respect and consideration for different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Taking responsibility, apologizing when mistakes are made, and learning from experience.
- Respecting other community members' time, effort, and contributions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and deliberate disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Threats, or encouraging violence or harm.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or community setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and other communication channels used for this project. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces or events.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers through the repository's issue or contact channels. Reports should include enough detail to understand what happened. Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly, respect the privacy of the reporter to the extent possible, and take any action they consider appropriate.

Project maintainers are expected to keep reports confidential where practical and to avoid retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may use the following response levels, depending on the severity and context of the behavior:

1. **Correction:** A private or public clarification explaining why the behavior is inappropriate and requesting that it stop.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning that the behavior is unacceptable and that further conduct may result in action.
3. **Temporary restriction:** A temporary restriction from interactions or project spaces for a specified period.
4. **Permanent restriction:** A permanent ban from project spaces for repeated or severe violations.

Maintainers will consider the nature, severity, frequency, and impact of each incident. They may choose an appropriate response and are not required to apply these steps sequentially.
